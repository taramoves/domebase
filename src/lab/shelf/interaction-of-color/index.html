“In visual perception a color is almost never seen as it really is — as it physically is. This fact makes color the most relative medium in art.”

Josef Albers, Interaction of Color p. 1

the course

Twenty-six studies, each one an exercise: the book teaches colour by setting a problem and letting the eye solve it. Sixty pages of plates carry the solutions the class found.
